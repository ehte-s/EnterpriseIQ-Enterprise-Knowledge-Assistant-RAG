# ACME CORPORATION — PROCUREMENT, TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY
Document ID: DOC-FIN-2026-V4
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Applicability: Global Enterprise Operations

1. CORPORATE CREDIT CARDS & PURCHASING THRESHOLDS
1.1 Tier 1 Purchases (Under $1,000 USD): May be approved directly by the employee's direct line manager using a designated Corporate Purchasing Card (P-Card).
1.2 Tier 2 Purchases ($1,000 to $10,000 USD): Require written approval from both the Department Director and the Financial Controller before issuing a formal Purchase Order (PO).
1.3 Tier 3 Purchases (Exceeding $10,000 USD): Require competitive bidding with at least three (3) qualified vendor quotes and final authorization from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
1.4 Software & SaaS Licenses: Any software tool, cloud subscription, or AI API access must undergo IT Security review and Data Privacy impact assessment prior to purchase, regardless of dollar value.

2. BUSINESS TRAVEL & ACCOMMODATION
2.1 Air Travel: Employees must book economy class for all domestic flights and international flights under 6 hours duration. Business class airfare is permitted only for international flights exceeding 8 continuous flight hours with prior VP approval.
2.2 Lodging: Hotel accommodations should not exceed standard corporate benchmark caps ($220/night in standard metropolitan areas; $350/night in high-cost cities such as New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, and Zurich).
2.3 Daily Meals & Incidentals (Per Diem):
    - Domestic Travel: Up to $75 USD per day for meals.
    - International Travel: Up to $110 USD per day for meals.
    - Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless associated with an approved client entertainment dinner.

3. EXPENSE SUBMISSION & AUDITING
3.1 Expense reports must be submitted via the Financial Cloud Portal within 30 calendar days of incurring the expense. Late submissions exceeding 60 days are subject to rejection.
3.2 Itemized receipts are strictly mandatory for any individual expense exceeding $25 USD. Credit card summary slips without itemization are invalid.
3.3 Non-Reimbursable Expenses: Personal grooming, traffic or parking violations, hotel mini-bar purchases, flight cancellation insurance, and spouse/companion travel costs are strictly personal expenses.
