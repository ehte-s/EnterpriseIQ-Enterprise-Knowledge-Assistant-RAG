# ACME CORPORATION — GLOBAL HUMAN RESOURCES & WORKPLACE POLICY
Document ID: DOC-HR-2026-V3
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Full-Time and Part-Time Employees Globally

1. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
1.1 All full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year, accrued at 2.08 days per completed month of active service.
1.2 Up to 5 unused annual leave days may be carried over into the following calendar year, provided they are consumed prior to March 31. Any additional unused days expire without financial compensation.
1.3 Leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive working days must be submitted through the Enterprise HR Portal at least 14 calendar days in advance for management approval.

2. SICK LEAVE & MEDICAL ABSENCE
2.1 Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year, allocated on January 1.
2.2 For sick leave absences lasting longer than three (3) consecutive working days, an official medical certificate signed by a licensed medical practitioner must be uploaded to the HR Portal within 48 hours of return to work.
2.3 Chronic health conditions requiring extended leave of absence are eligible for Short-Term Disability (STD) coverage beginning on the 11th consecutive day of medical absence, providing 70% of base salary for up to 26 weeks.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE & FAMILY CARE
3.1 Primary Caregiver Leave: Any eligible employee who becomes a parent via birth, adoption, or surrogacy is entitled to 16 consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave at 100% of regular base salary.
3.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave: Eligible secondary caregivers are entitled to 6 consecutive weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be taken within the first 12 months following birth or legal adoption.
3.3 Bereavement Leave: Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive paid working days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and 3 days for extended family members.

4. REMOTE WORK & HYBRID SCHEDULE
4.1 The standard working policy operates on a 3:2 hybrid model: employees are required to work on-site at a designated regional office a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday are designated core collaboration days).
4.2 Fully remote work arrangements require formal approval from the Department Head and the Vice President of People Operations.
4.3 Remote employees receive a one-time home office equipment stipend of $750 upon hire, plus a recurring monthly internet and mobile connectivity stipend of $60.

5. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS & COMPENSATION
5.1 Annual performance reviews occur during Q4 (October 15 - November 30), with merit-based compensation adjustments taking effect on February 1 of the subsequent year.
5.2 Annual bonuses are determined based on a weighted 60% company OKR achievement factor and 40% individual performance score.
